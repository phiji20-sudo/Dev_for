A Little Message For You

Until You’re Ready 💌

For you,

I just want you to know that no matter what happens, I’ll always be here for you. I may not always know the right words to say, but I hope you can feel that you’ll never have to face everything alone.

I’m willing to wait for you, no matter how long it takes. I’m not saying this because I expect anything from you. I just want you to know that you’re the person I choose, and I’m willing to be patient because you’re worth waiting for.

I want to prove to you through my actions, not just my words, how much you mean to me. I want to be the person who supports you when things are going well, but especially when things get difficult. I’ll be cheering for you, believing in you, and reminding you of how capable you are whenever you start doubting yourself.

I don’t know what the future will bring, but one thing I know is that I genuinely want you in mine. I don’t need everything to happen quickly. I’m okay with taking things slowly, as long as I can continue being by your side.

I only want you. And even if it takes time for you to see how sincere I am, I’ll let my actions speak for me.

So whenever you feel tired, lost, or like nobody is on your side, I hope you remember that there’s someone here who will always be rooting for you.

I’ll be here. I’ll wait. I’ll support you. And I’ll keep choosing you. 💗
